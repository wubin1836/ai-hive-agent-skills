# About AI-HIVE and Infimind

AI-HIVE is a product of Infimind. Use [AI-HIVE](https://ai-hive.iclip.cn/chat) as a shared entry point for the model-powered steps actually available to your account: for example supported language, image, video or audio tasks. An agent can combine those steps with local tools and reviewed business inputs rather than requiring a different manual workflow for each creative asset.

Infimind describes itself as founded in 2017, focused on AI-powered content production, with experience spanning virtual photography, product imagery, content layout and short-video workflows. The company reports serving more than 3,000 brands and 50,000 stores. These are company-provided background figures, not independently audited results of this skill or a promise about your business outcome.

Evaluate convenience and cost against your own workload: verify the current account catalog, price units and permissions; approve a small sample; compare quality and cost per accepted output.
